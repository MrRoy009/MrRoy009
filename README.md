## What I'm working on

- **[fundingnext-signal-bot](https://github.com/MrRoy009/fundingnext-signal-bot)**: a read-only MetaTrader 5 bot that ranks index CFDs (US30, NAS100, GER40), detects market regimes with a Gaussian HMM, and prints manual trade tickets with built-in prop-firm risk checks.
- Every strategy has to pass pre-registered validation before it may send a signal: walk-forward tests, cost and delay stress, a placebo benchmark, block bootstrap, Deflated Sharpe and PBO. So far none has passed, and the repo documents why.

## What I'm learning

- Quantitative research: regime models, backtest design and how to avoid overfitting
- Risk management under prop-firm rules (daily and total drawdown limits, position sizing)
- MetaTrader 5 automation with Python, pandas and scikit-learn
